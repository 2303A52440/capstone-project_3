🚀 Excited to unveil our Capstone Project at SR University: 
"Autonomous Collaborative Robot Swarm Simulator & Digital Twin (MAPF / CBS)" 🤖📦

In modern high-density fulfillment centers, coordinating dozens of Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) without deadlocks or collisions is a classic NP-hard challenge. Traditional coupled A* methods explode exponentially O(|V|^N), while prioritized planning leads to costly detours and livelocks.

To solve this, our team—Sanjay, Sathwik, and Seetharam—engineered an end-to-end Multi-Agent Pathfinding (MAPF) system driven by:
✅ Two-Level Conflict-Based Search (CBS) resolving spatial vertex and edge-swap collisions.
✅ Low-Level 3D Space-Time A* Search over (x, y, t) manifolds with Manhattan distance heuristics.
✅ Standalone Zero-Dependency Python Desktop Application (Tkinter GUI).
✅ Web-Based Interactive Digital Twin (HTML5 Canvas & TailwindCSS) with <1.0ms dynamic floor replanning.
✅ Real-Time ROS2 / MQTT Telemetry Bridge emitting JSON payloads on /agv_telemetry.

Benchmark results show a 100% collision-free guarantee with exact Sum-of-Costs (SOC) optimality and sub-millisecond route recalculation upon dynamic obstacle placement!

We owe deep gratitude to our project guide Dr. Sudha Rani, Head of School Dr. CH. Sandeep, Dean Dr. M. Sheshikala, and coordinator Mr. Sallauddin Md at SR University School of Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence for their invaluable mentorship.
